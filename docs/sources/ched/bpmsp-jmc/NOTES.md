## Part I, general provisions
**Section 1, scope.**
- A merit-based program for students whose parents or legal guardians have a combined annual gross income **not exceeding ₱2,000,000**.
- Two tracks:
  - **(a) Higher Education Track:** CHED-recognized undergraduate degree programs at qualified HEIs.
  - **(b) TVET Diploma Track:** TESDA priority diploma programs at TESDA-registered TVIs.

**Section 2, general eligibility.**
- **2.1 HE Track:** an incoming college student who graduated from Grade 12 and **belongs to the Top Five (5) of the graduating class OR has a GWA of at least 95%** (or its equivalent; footnote 3: adjustments need Commission En Banc approval), **and has not earned any units or degree in tertiary education.**
- **2.1 TVET track:** SHS GWA of at least 90%; must not hold a tech-voc diploma or NC III or higher (unless it was part of SHS).
- **2.1.1 Both tracks:** parents' or guardians' combined gross income **not over ₱2,000,000**.
- **2.2 Also eligible for the HE track, *irrespective of age*:** holders of TESDA NC I–IV, SHS tech-pro graduates, and employed people seeking higher education, as long as they have no tertiary units or degree and meet 2.1.
- **2.3 Old-curriculum graduates** (4th Year High School on or before SY 2015–2016) may apply if:
  1. they have no higher-education units;
  2. their 4th-year final average is used;
  3. they meet the income and admission requirements.
- **2.4** The applicant must sign a **mandatory return-service undertaking (Annex B-2)**, with priority to government and public institutions.

**Section 3, ineligibility.**
| | Who is ineligible |
|---|---|
| 3.1 | Foreign applicants, or **dual citizens** |
| 3.2 | Incomplete documents by the deadline |
| 3.3 | Incomplete, falsified or tampered documents |
| 3.4 | Already took or earned undergraduate units, or completed a degree |
| 3.5 | **Will repeat or re-enter the first year** of college |
| 3.6 | **Transferees or shiftees with credited units** |
| 3.7 | Existing recipients of any **nationally government-funded** scholarship or grant. **Excluded from this ban:** grants from private HEIs, LGUs, LUCs, private organizations, and one-time community or local grants |
| 3.8 | **A pending case or record of disciplinary action in school or employment.** Declared through the portal; CHED may require an Honorable Dismissal. **3.8.1:** misrepresentation is a ground for termination (18.1.2) |

**Section 4, general documents.**
- **4.1** Application form on the **official BPMSP online portal**.
- **4.2** PSA birth certificate.
- **4.3** Certified true copy of Form 138 / SF9, signed by the SHS registrar.
- **4.4** Any one proof of income:
  - (a) BIR Certificate of Tax Exemption or Non-Filer;
  - (b) DSWD or C/MSWD certificate for **4Ps** beneficiaries;
  - (c) latest ITR (BIR Form 1701) or BIR Form 2316 of both parents or guardians, or of the applicant;
  - (d) certified true copy of the latest contract or proof of income (OFW or seafarer).
- **4.5** Extra proof for **special equity groups**.

**Footnote 5, special equity groups** (extra ranking points):
- (a) Indigenous Peoples (RA 8371);
- (b) PWDs (RA 7277);
- (c) **solo parents and their dependents** (RA 8972 / 11861);
- (d) **persons living in poverty** (RA 11291 / 8425);
- (e) underprivileged and homeless citizens (RA 7279);
- (f) senior citizens (RA 9994);
- (g) others such as **first-generation students**.

## Part II, Higher Education Track
**Section 5, specific eligibility.**
- **5.1** The applicant must be officially enrolled (see 8.4.3) in an **identified priority program** at a Philippine HEI whose program is:
  - (a) a CHED Center of Excellence (COE) or Center of Development (COD); or
  - (b) Level III–IV accredited; or
  - (c) approved by the Commission En Banc.
- **5.1.1** The priority list is approved by the CEB **every academic year**.
- **5.2** LUCs need the latest CHED Institutional Recognition.
- **5.3** HEIs need a **MOA with CHEDRO** as a Delivering HEI (DHEI).

**Section 6, HE documents.**
- **6.1** College admission slip or proof of admission.
- **6.2** Parent or Legal Guardian Consent Form (**Annex L**), if applicable.
- **6.3** Old-curriculum applicants: 4th Year Report Card (Form 138).
- **6.4** Certified SHS certificate of **Top Five** of the class, if applicable.

**Section 7, privileges.**
- **7.1** TOSF, stipend and allowance, and the Academic Excellence Incentive **until the program is completed, provided the scholar passes all subjects every term.** Bridging programs are not covered.

| Benefit | SUCs / LUCs (DNSC) | Private HEIs |
|---|---|---|
| TOSF | Covered by the UAQTE Act (Free Higher Education) | up to ₱45,000 per AY |
| **Stipend and allowance** | **₱45,000 per AY** (₱22,500 per semester; ₱15,000 per trimester; ₱11,250 per quarter) | same |
| **Academic Excellence Incentive** | **₱18,000 per term, for the top 15 scholars per region** (by GWA; ties go to special equity, then lower income) | same |

All amounts are subject to the GAA and CEB approval (7.1.2).

**Section 8, application and selection.**
- **8.2** CHED and DepEd issue the call through the **BPMSP online portal**.
- **8.3** Submit through the portal. Applicants from **GIDCAs** use the paper form (Annex A). There is one chance to resubmit deficient documents. **8.3.1:** coursed through the CHEDRO where the DHEI is.
- **8.4** CHEDRO shortlists by completeness and eligibility.
  - **8.4.3** Applicants without an admission slip yet are provisionally accepted and have **1 month from the start of the semester** to submit proof of enrollment.
- **8.5** Eligible applicants get a **Notice of Award**. Those with a pending admission slip get a **Letter of Guarantee**.
- **8.6 Scoring:** **Final Score = (GWA × 70%) + (Income Points × 30%) + additional points.**
- **8.7 a. Academic points (SHS GWA):** 100–99 = 100; 98–97 = 90; 96–95 = 80; 94–93 = 70; 92–91 = 60; 90 and below = 50.
- **8.7 b. Income points:** ≤₱500k = 100; ₱500,001–750k = 90; 750,001–1M = 80; 1,000,001–1.25M = 70; 1,250,001–1.5M = 60; 1,500,001–1.75M = 50; 1,750,001–2M = 40.
- **8.7 c.** **+10 points** for any special equity group. This is the maximum, even for several groups. Proof:
  - PWD ID or PDAO certification;
  - Solo Parent ID (applicant or parent);
  - Senior Citizen ID;
  - Social Case Study (first-generation);
  - NCIP certification.
- **8.8** Award by ranking and regional slots.
  - **8.8.1 Tie-breaks:** (a) resides in a GIDCA; (b) has a PWD parent; (c) Grade 12 Leadership Award or an award for outstanding performance (DepEd DO 36, s. 2016).
- **8.9–8.11** CHEDRO validates enrollment in a priority program and informs applicants in writing.
- **8.12–8.13** NOA (Annex B); **accept or waive within 15 days**.
- **8.14** If qualified for **two or more national scholarships, the applicant must choose one**. Failing to confirm is a waiver of BPMSP.
- **8.15** Return the signed NOA within 15 working days.
- **8.16–8.17** Scholarship Agreement (Annex B-1); CHEDRO informs DepEd and the DHEI.

**Section 9, slots.**
- **9.1** Shared between SUCs/LUCs and private HEIs.
- **9.2 Priority domains:**
  1. Agriculture and Fisheries
  2. Creative Industries
  3. Healthcare and Allied Programs
  4. Engineering and Technology
  5. Financial Services
  6. Teacher Education
  7. Digital Technology
  8. Science and Mathematics

  The CEB may update this list.
- **9.3** Distributed by CHEDRO, by the number of qualified DHEIs.
- **9.4** Unused slots go back to OSDS.

**Section 10, payment.**
- The stipend and incentive are paid **directly to the scholar's bank account**; TOSF is paid to the DHEI.

| Payment | Documents |
|---|---|
| **Initial** | Certified COR/COE; college or government ID with signature, or a school certification; photocopy of an **active Landbank ATM card** (if applicable); HEI certification of no other national scholarship |
| **Succeeding** | Certified COR/COE; certified grades with the previous-term GWA; HEI certification of no other national scholarship (every start of the AY) |

- **10.2.3** TOSF is paid to the DHEI, never as cash. SUC/LUC scholars are covered by Free Higher Education.
- **10.3** Liquidation within 30 working days. **10.3.3:** the DHEI must keep the scholar enrolled even while liquidation is pending.

## Part III, TVET Diploma Track (TESDA)
Sections 11–12: enrolled or admitted in a TESDA-registered diploma program. Proof: a Certificate of Acceptance, Training Agreement, or Enrollment Confirmation. **Not modelled in BAKR.**

## Part V, common provisions
**18.1 Termination.**
- **Effects:** all benefits stop; repay under Section 19; removed from the roster; ineligibility under Section 3 means **permanent disqualification**.
- **18.1.2 Grounds:**
  - violating the JMC;
  - breach of the agreement;
  - later found ineligible;
  - **availing of another national scholarship**;
  - **transferring or shifting outside the CHED priority domain** (within the domain needs CHEDRO approval);
  - **failing any subject in a semester**;
  - not enrolling in the regular load;
  - dropping out or AWOL;
  - **an LOA of more than 1 AY**;
  - acts inimical to the government;
  - hazing;
  - violating HEI rules.
- **18.1.3** Notice of Termination (Annex K); appeal **within 7 working days**; decision in 15 working days; Mitigating Circumstances Committee; then the CEB.
- **18.1.4** Benefits are suspended while an appeal is pending.

**18.2 Replacement.**
- Taken from the Official Ranklist (NOA not accepted within 15 days, or a scholar terminated).
- Replacements get only the remaining semesters, with no retroactive benefits.

**18.3 LOA.** **Once**, for up to **1 AY**, for valid reasons (health, family, academic, calamity). Approved by CHEDRO or TESDA (Annex H). No benefits during the LOA.

**18.4 Extension.**
- "Irregular" status if below a full load (except when finishing the last subjects).
- An extension beyond the program length is only for exceptional cases (Annex H).

**18.5 Mandatory return service.**
- **1 year of service for each year (or fraction) of support**, in the Philippines.
- Aligned with the field of study.
- Starts **within 1 year of graduation**.
- Graduate studies are allowed first only for medicine, guidance and counselling, or forensic medicine, in the Philippines, for at most 4 years.
- Board or licensure exams must be taken within 1 year.

## How BAKR uses it
**Required rules:**
- citizen (no dual citizens);
- incoming first-year, not repeating first year;
- Top 5 **or** GWA of at least 95%;
- no units;
- no degree;
- income ₱2M or less;
- no national grant (private, LGU and one-time grants allowed);
- no disciplinary record;
- priority program (*confirm*).

**Optional:** special equity group (raises the score, like the +10).
