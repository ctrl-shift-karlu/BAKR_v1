# Revised TES Guidelines of 2026 (CHED Tertiary Education Subsidy) + unifast.gov.ph/tes.html

- **Issuer:** CHED – UniFAST Board, under Section 7 of RA 10931 (the UAQTE Act)
- **Effective:** 1st Semester AY 2026–2027 and onwards (Rationale)
- **Pages:** 12 (screenshots `page-01`…`page-14`), plus `website-unifast-tes.png` (the UniFAST TES web page, cropped)
- **Used for:** `ched-tes`
- **Read in full:** 2026-11-15

## Section 1, coverage
- Higher-education students. **TVET TES is governed by TESDA's own guidelines.**
- Schools covered: SUCs, CHED-recognized LUCs and private HEIs with a CHED-UniFAST MOA and program compliance (COPC or RRPA; IR or GR).

## Section 3, student prioritization (the key section)
New slots are allocated **in this order, subject to funds**:

1. **Enrolled students from households in the latest Listahanan.**
   - If Listahanan is discontinued (as certified by DSWD), the **4Ps list** is used.
   - Within this group, dependents of solo parents, Indigenous Peoples and PWDs are included.
   - Ranked by household per capita income (DSWD).
2. **"All other enrolled students … not part of the prioritization category in item no. 1, … as follows":**
   - **2.1 Dependents of solo parents** (RA 11861): unmarried, unemployed, aged 22 or below (or older if unable to care for themselves). Must have **active status as dependents in the official DSWD registry**.
   - **2.2 Indigenous Cultural Communities / Indigenous Peoples** (RA 8371): must be in the **NCIP list** of officially recognized households.

> **How BAKR reads this:** only these groups are covered by new TES slots. A student in none of them (Listahanan or 4Ps, solo-parent dependent, or IP) is not covered. **PWD alone is not a group.**

## Section 4, amount of subsidy
| Group | Amount |
|---|---|
| Section 3(1), SUC or LUC | **₱10,000 per semester / ₱20,000 per AY** |
| Section 3(1), private HEI | ₱13,500 per semester / ₱27,000 per AY |
| **TES-3A, PWD** | **an additional ₱5,000 per semester / ₱10,000 per AY** |
| **TES-3B, licensure exam graduates** | one-time reimbursement of **up to ₱8,000** |
| Sections 3(2.1) and 3(2.2) | **₱5,000 per semester / ₱10,000 per AY**, ranked by household per capita income |

Benefits are **not reduced** and continue for the whole undergraduate program, as long as the grantee keeps meeting the eligibility and retention requirements (subject to funds).

## Section 5, SARDO (students at risk of dropping out)
- Identified through DepEd's Dropout Reduction Program (DO 74, s. 2010).
- **One-time ₱10,000**, subject to the HEI's evaluation of financial risk (death or illness of the main earner, calamities, crises).
- Separate guidelines apply.

## Section 6, TES for PNSL
Private HEIs in towns with no SUC or LUC, at least 20 km away, or within 20 km under conditions. Not relevant to DNSC.

## Section 7, ineligibility
**A. New applicants.**
1. Foreign citizens.
2. **Non-students.**
3. Students in HEIs or programs not in the CHED Registry.
4. Students **already in a second undergraduate program**.
5. **Existing TDP, CHED scholarship or other national StuFAP beneficiaries.** Recipients of a **one-time grant** (AICS, SMART and similar) are exempt.
6. Spurious documents.

**B. Continuing grantees.**
- No enrollment for 1 term without an LOA, or for 2 terms in an AY.
- Suspension or expulsion.
- Failing the HEI's retention requirements.
- Not finishing within **one year after the prescribed period**.
- Spurious documents.

## Section 8, duration of eligibility
- **The standard length of the program plus one year**, counted from the AY the TES award number is issued.
- LOA periods are excluded.
- Transferees: based on the original program plus 1 year. If the new program is shorter, the shorter length is used.

## Sections 10–11, validation and documents
**11.A Application:** after the UniFAST call, **the HEI submits Annex 1, the Enrolled Students Applicant List, to the UniFAST Portal.**

**11.B New grantees.**
- **B.1, the HEI submits:** Annex 2 (Forms 1–3), a notarized Registrar's Certificate, and COR/COE (PDF).
- **B.2, the student submits directly** (if the HEI fails): COR/COE (PDF), and a student ID or government ID with specimen signature (PDF).
- **B.3, PWDs:** a **PWD ID from the PDAO** (PDF).

**11.C Continuing grantees.** The same as TDP:
- the Enrollment List or COR/COE;
- returning grantees: COR/COE and the LOA;
- transferees: previous and current COR/COE (plus a Certificate of Residency for PNSL grantees before AY 2026–2027);
- the direct route: COR/COE, ID and LOA.

## Sections 12–16
- **Billing and disbursement:** the same as TDP: LDDAP-ADA, then check, then the HEI; paid **within 15 working days**; unclaimed returned in 90 days.
- 1% management fee (excluding TES-3A, TES-3B and SARDO), liquidation, sanctions, and monitoring.

## Website (unifast.gov.ph/tes.html, "Starting Fiscal Year 2023")
- **Eligibility:** Filipino citizen; meets the SUC or LUC admission and retention policies; no previous undergraduate degree; not overstaying (for example 4 or 5 years plus a 1-year grace).
- **Benefits:** SUC or LUC ₱20,000 per AY; private HEI ₱27,000 per AY; TES-3A PWD ₱10,000 per AY; TES-3B ₱8,000 one-time.
- **Documents:** COR/COE; Certificate of Residency (private, no-SUC/LUC category); PWD ID if applicable.
- *"Subject to prioritization and the availability of funds."*

## How BAKR uses it
**Required rules:**
- citizen;
- enrolled;
- no previous degree;
- within the normal years plus 1;
- no other national aid (one-time grants exempt);
- **in a TES group** (Listahanan, 4Ps, solo-parent dependent, or IP).

**Optional:** admission and retention (*confirm*).
