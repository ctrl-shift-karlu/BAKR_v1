# Revised TDP Guidelines of 2026 (CHED Tulong Dunong Program)

- **Issuer:** CHED – UniFAST Board
- **Authority:** UniFAST Board Resolution No. 2026-012, 13th Special Meeting, approved **16 April 2026**, signed **3 June 2026**
- **Effective:** 1st Semester AY 2026–2027 (Section 15)
- **Pages:** 8 (screenshots `page-01`…`page-11`)
- **Used for:** `ched-tdp` in `config/scholarships.php`
- **Read in full:** 2026-11-15

## Who is covered
**Section 1, coverage.** The guidelines cover SUCs, CHED-recognized LUCs and private HEIs that:
- have a MOA with CHED-UniFAST;
- have all their programs covered by COPC (or RRPA for new programs), IR or GR.

**Section 2, qualifications.** The applicant must be a **Filipino citizen** and one of:
- **2.1 New TDP applicant:** enrolled in a **first undergraduate degree** at a school in the CHED Registry with a UniFAST MOA.
- **2.2 New grantee for 1st Sem AY 2026–2027:** gets that semester only, unless funding continues in later fiscal years.
- **2.3 Continuing grantee:** was a TDP beneficiary before AY 2026–2027, has a TDP Award Number, and is enrolled.

## The grant
**Section 3, grant amount.**
- **₱7,500 per semester (₱15,000 per academic year)**, for both continuing and new grantees.
- A new grantee's amount may be increased if the UniFAST Board approves.

## Ineligibility and termination (Section 4)
| | Ground |
|---|---|
| 4.1 | Fails the admission or retention policies of the HEI |
| 4.2 | Does not enroll for 1 term with no Leave of Absence (LOA) |
| 4.3 | Does not enroll for at least 2 terms in an academic year |
| 4.4 | Does not tell CHEDRO about dropping out, deferment or transfer |
| 4.5 | Suspended by the HEI for 1 term or more |
| 4.6 | Expelled |
| 4.7 | Does not finish the degree within **one year after the prescribed period** (Maximum Residency Rule, MRR) |
| 4.8 | Submitted spurious documents |
| 4.9 | Receives multiple national government-funded aid, **except** Free Higher Education (RA 10931) |

**More on 4.9:**
- Recipients of a **one-time grant** (AICS of DSWD, SMART, or similar) may still apply, and continuing grantees are not delisted for it.
- **TDP and SMART cannot be received in the same semester.**

## Application (Section 5)
- **5.1** The UniFAST Secretariat issues a call **after the HEI's enrollment period**.
  - Applications go through the partner implementer (the HEI) or directly to CHEDRO.
  - Continuing grantees do **not** re-apply (except transferees). They are validated under Section 6.2.
- **5.2** CHEDRO uploads applications to the RC Portal.
- **5.3** Eligibility cross-matching against duplicate grants. CHEDRO releases the list of qualified applicants to the HEI.
- **5.4** The HEI submits the documents to CHEDRO. If it does not, the applicant may submit them directly.

## Validation (Section 6)
- **6.1** New applicants are validated by CHEDRO, which issues a Master List of Grantees. The **MRR replaces the maximum-load requirement.**
- **6.2** Continuing grantees are validated with the COR/COE (PDF) or the Enrollment List.
- **6.3** Returning after a 1-term LOA: a certified e-generated COR/COE plus a certified copy of the approved LOA.
- **6.4** Transferees: a certified e-generated COR/COE. They may only transfer to schools in the CHED Registry.

## Documents (Section 7)
**A. New grantees.**
1. Scanned TDP Application Form.
2. COR/COE (PDF).
3. **Certificate of Indigency** from the Punong Barangay where the applicant lives. **Only if it cannot be secured:** a Certificate of Tax Exemption, or a Social Case Study by a registered social worker.

**B. Continuing grantees.**
- **B.1, the HEI submits:** Annex 2 (Forms 1–3), a notarized Registrar's Certificate, and the Enrollment List (or COR/COE PDF).
  - Returning grantee: COR/COE and the LOA (PDF).
  - Transferee: COR/COE of the previous and current semester (PDF).
- **B.2, the grantee submits directly** (if the HEI fails): COR/COE (PDF), an ID with specimen signature (PDF), and the LOA approved by the HEI (as needed).

## Payment (Sections 8–10)
**Billing (8.1):** only grantees on the Master List are paid.

**Disbursement order (8-II A):**
1. Direct credit (LDDAP-ADA).
2. Check.
3. Through the HEI.

**Time limits:**
- HEIs or CHEDROs pay students **within 15 working days** of receiving funds.
- Unclaimed grants are returned **within 90 days**.
- Representative claimants and deceased grantees have their own document lists.

**Fees and reporting:**
- **Section 9:** a 1% management fee, split between the HEI and CHEDRO.
- **Section 10:** liquidation within 90 days.

## Other sections
- **Section 11:** sanctions on HEIs.
- **Section 12:** doubts are resolved in favour of the deserving grantee.
- **Sections 13–14:** separability and repeal.

## How BAKR uses it
- **Rules:** citizen; first degree; enrolled; MRR (skipped for incoming first-years); no other national aid (one-time grants and SMART noted); Certificate of Indigency (*confirm*, required); retention and call open (*confirm*, optional).
- **No income limit.** The document has none; need is shown by the indigency certificate.
