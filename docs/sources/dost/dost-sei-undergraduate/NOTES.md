## Types of scholarship
- **Merit Scholarship** (formerly the NSDB or NSTA Scholarship): for students with high aptitude in science and mathematics who are willing to pursue S&T careers.
- **RA 7687 Scholarship** (Science and Technology Scholarship Act of 1994): for talented and deserving students whose families' socio-economic status does **not exceed the set cut-off values of certain indicators**. Qualifiers must take priority fields: basic sciences, engineering, other applied sciences, and science and mathematics teaching.

## Scholarship privileges
**A. Regular academic year.**
| Privilege | Amount |
|---|---|
| Tuition and other school fees (scholars in private HEIs) | actual fees, up to ₱40,000 per AY |
| Learning materials and / or connectivity allowance | ₱10,000 per AY |
| **Monthly living allowance** | **₱8,000 per month** |
| Clothing allowance | ₱1,000 (1st semester of 1st year only) |
| Transportation (studying outside the home province) | 1 economy-class round-trip fare |
| Group accident and health insurance | premium |
| Thesis allowance | ₱10,000 |
| Research dissemination grant* | not over ₱50,000 |
| Graduation allowance | ₱1,000 |

\* Applied for by the scholar, subject to evaluation and approval.

**B. Mid-year allowance** (if the curriculum requires it):
- tuition and other school fees, actual, up to ₱10,000;
- learning materials ₱2,000;
- living allowance ₱8,000 per month.

## Study placement
- University of the Philippines.
- **State universities and colleges** (DNSC qualifies).
- Private institutions recognized by CHED as **Centers of Excellence or Development**, or with **FAAP Level III accreditation**, for the priority S&T programs.
