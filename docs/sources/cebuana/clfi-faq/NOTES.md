- **Issuer:** Cebuana Lhuillier Foundation (cebuanafoundation.org, FAQ section)
- **Screenshots:** `page-01`…`page-04`
- **Used for:** `cebuana-clfi`, together with the foundation's qualifications page read on 2026-11-07

| # | Question | Official answer (summary, faithful) |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Open for elementary or high school? | **No.** Grants are for **college education, 1st to 4th year**, for **economically challenged** students nationwide |
| Q2 | Qualifications | **Cebuana Lhuillier client with a 24K Loyalty Card**; proof(s) of total family income; **GWA of 85%** or its equivalent from the last semester or school attended, **without a failing mark in any subject** (grades from the 2nd semester of SY 2025–2026; **freshmen may use SHS grades**); Certificate of Good Moral Character signed by an official of the last school; current enrollment or registration form (**1st semester SY 2026–2027**); the university's or college's grading system in percentages. Apply through the **Scholarship Form tab** (online form) |
| Q3 | Private university? | **No.** Applicants must apply to, or be enrolled in, a **public state university or college** that offers a supported course |
| Q4 | After submission | CLFI does a **paper screening** with departments of the **PJ Group of Companies**. Successful applicants are called to an **interview with their parents or guardians**. Official results are out **on or before the last week of October 2026** |
| Q5 | Stipend | A **fixed stipend of up to ₱50,000** for essential school-related and living expenses |
| Q6 | Other benefits | Annual general assembly; **monthly learning sessions** with resource persons or experts; outreach programs; **Education-to-Employment Program**; various trainings; Cebuana Lhuillier Museum tour; membership in the Cebuana Lhuillier Scholar Alumni Association; **leadership boot camp**; recognition of academic and non-academic achievements |
| Q7 | Jobs after graduation? | **Yes**, through the **Education to Employment (E2E)** provision. **PJLI waives its pre-employment test** for scholars who graduate with **Latin honors or a GWA of 90%** or its equivalent. Others go through the standard process |
| Q8 | Appeal? | **No.** The committee's decision is **final and irrevocable**. Applicants may **reapply in later years** (the call is annual) |
| Q9 | My course is not on the list: is that non-negotiable? | **Yes.** Only the selected courses aligned with the E2E provision are accepted |
| Q10 | Siblings in the same school year? | **No.** Only **one child per household at a time** |
| Q11 | Incoming 3rd year? | **Yes.** Open to **all year levels** |
| Q12 | SHS report card not yet released? | Request a **certification from the registrar showing the Grade 12 GWA** |

## Supported courses
From the qualifications page, 2026-11-07: Accountancy / Finance; Business; Hospitality; Electrical, Civil, Mechanical and Industrial Engineering; Architecture; Psychology; IT.

## Not in the FAQ (from the 2026-11-07 qualifications page; needs re-checking)
- **Total annual family income of ₱350,000 or less.** The FAQ only says "economically challenged" and "proof of total family income".
- **A Cebuana Lhuillier Microsavings account.** Not in the FAQ's Q2, so it is no longer required in BAKR.
- **Whether a relative's 24K Loyalty Card counts.** The FAQ says "Cebuana Lhuillier client with 24K Loyalty Card" only.

## How BAKR uses it
**Required rules:**
- citizen;
- client with a 24K card (self = pass; relative only = *confirm*);
- public college, any year;
- supported course (`program_in`): BSIT = pass; BSIS, Entrepreneurship and Tourism = *confirm*; others = fail (Q9);
- income ₱350,000 or less;
- GWA of at least 85%;
- no failing mark.

**Optional:** Microsavings.

**Note only (not a rule):** one child per household.
