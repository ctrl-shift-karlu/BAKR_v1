# GrabScholar College Scholarship: "6 Merit-Based Scholarships for SY 2026-2030"

- **Issuer:** Grab Philippines (grab.com/ph, GrabScholar College Scholarship page, in Filipino)
- **Screenshots:** `page-01`…`page-05`
- **Used for:** `grabscholar`
- **Read in full:** 2026-11-15

## Scope (page 1)
- **Six** full-ride merit scholarships for **up to four years**, with allowances.
- For **incoming first-year** college students in **AY 2026–2030**.
- For students enrolling in **Business, STEM (except medical courses) and Sustainability** courses at **any university or college in the Philippines**.
- The scholarship is now open to all colleges and universities nationwide.
- Scholars are connected to **training, mentorship, internships and possible employment at Grab**.

## Details (pages 2–3)
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Number of scholarships | **6** |
| What "full-ride merit-based" means | School-related fees for the whole **3- to 4-year** degree are covered, **as long as the retention requirements are met** |
| Which schools | **Any university or college in the Philippines** |
| Which courses | 3- to 4-year Business, STEM (excluding medical) and Sustainability courses. The list is **"kasama—pero hindi limitado"** (included but **not limited** to) and ends **"At marami pang iba!"** (and many more) |
| Who may apply | Open to all **Filipino citizens**; **incoming freshmen or first-year college students** in any school in **AY 2026–2030** |
| Qualifications | Filipino citizen; **no other scholarship or educational assistance**; **Grade 12 GPA of 90% or higher**; **no disciplinary sanction in senior high school** |
| Benefits, for the whole degree | **100% tuition and miscellaneous fees**; **school supplies allowance (uniform, textbooks, student ID) of ₱11,000 a year**; **monthly student allowance of ₱8,000**; learning and development programs; internship opportunities |
| How applicants learn the result | **Only selected applicants are notified by SMS and e-mail.** The list of selected applicants is posted on the GrabScholar website on **August 20, 2026** |

**Course list.**
- **STEM:** Computer Science, Data Science, Data Analytics, **Information Technology**, Electrical Engineering, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, **Information Systems**, Cybersecurity.
- **Business:** Business Administration / Management, Finance, Marketing, Management, Operations Management, Human Resource, **Entrepreneurship**, Accounting, Economics.
- **Sustainability:** Sustainable Development, Environmental Science, Community Development, Urban Planning and Sustainability, Corporate Social Responsibility, Development Studies, Environmental Engineering, **Agroforestry**.
