## Source
- **Document:** Revised TDP Guidelines of 2026 (pages 1–8, sent by the owner as screenshots).
- **Authority:** UniFAST Board Resolution No. 2026-012, 13th Special Meeting, April 16, 2026; signed June 3, 2026.
- **Effective:** 1st Semester AY 2026–2027.
- **Replaces:** the earlier chedscholar.org reading from 2026-11-07, a third-party site whose page mixed 2026 and 2027.

## What changed in `config/scholarships.php` (ched-tdp only)
| | Before (chedscholar.org) | Now (official guidelines) |
|---|---|---|
| Income | **Required: ₱400,000 or less** | **Removed.** The guidelines publish no income cap. Need is shown with a Certificate of Indigency (Section 7-A), so it is now a required *confirm* rule. |
| Maximum Residency Rule | not checked | Required: within the program's normal length plus one year (Sections 4.7 and 6.1). It uses the existing "within the normal length" question and is skipped for incoming first-years. |
| Other government aid | "TES, a CHED scholarship, or another national student aid" | The same check, with the guidelines' exceptions named: Free Higher Education and one-time grants such as AICS do not count (Section 4.9). |
| Admission / retention | not checked | Optional *confirm* rule (Section 4.1). |
| Slots | "DNSC has TDP slots" | "A TDP call is open at DNSC this semester": the call comes after the enrollment period (Section 5.1). |
| Benefits | ₱7,500/semester, plus a list of uses | ₱7,500 per semester (₱15,000 per academic year) for new and continuing grantees; may be increased by the UniFAST Board; continuing grantees do not re-apply (Sections 3 and 5.1). The list of uses was removed because it is not in the guidelines. |
| Documents | 5 items, including "number of enrolled units" | The 3 items in Section 7-A: Scanned TDP Application Form, COR/COE (PDF), and Certificate of Indigency from the Punong Barangay (alternatives: Certificate of Tax Exemption or a Social Case Study). The units certificate was dropped, because the MRR replaces the maximum-load check. |
| How to apply | "through the school" | The Section 5 process: UniFAST call → DNSC or CHEDRO → RC Portal → duplicate cross-matching → list released to DNSC. |
| Official link | chedscholar.org (third party) | unifast.gov.ph |
| Checked on | 2026-11-07 | 2026-11-15 (per-scholarship `verified_on`; `ScholarshipSeeder` now honours it) |

With these rules, TDP shows as **Nearly eligible** at best: the indigency certificate and the open call can only be confirmed with the provider. That is accurate, because CHED cross-checks applicants and releases the final list.

## New: `php artisan bakr:sync-scholarship {slug} [--apply]`
- **What it does:** updates ONE scholarship from config without touching the others or their provider edits (unlike `ScholarshipSeeder`).
- **Records it:** writes a `scholarship_revisions` row with user = none, meaning a system update from the official source.
- **Re-checks matches:** re-runs every student's current matches when the rules change.
- **Safe by default:** without `--apply` it only previews.

Preview on the real `bakr` DB, read-only:
- 9 fields differ;
- rule `income` removed;
- rules `mrr`, `indigency` and `retention` added;
- 4 student forms to re-check.

**Not applied yet:** waiting for the owner's go-ahead.

# CHED Merit Scholarship Program (CMSP) updated from CMO No. 13, s. 2025

## Source
- **Document:** CHED Memorandum Order No. 13, s. 2025, "Revised Policies and Guidelines for the CHED Merit Scholarship Program (CMSP)".
- **Authority:** CHED en banc Resolution No. 498-2025, 30 June 2025.
- **Pages seen:** Art. I; Sections 4–6, 13–17 and 28 (sent as screenshots by the owner on 2026-11-15).
- **Settles the earlier pasted text:** that text claimed 96% and ₱400,000 with no source. It was wrong; the CMO says **93% and ₱500,000**.

## Confirmed (unchanged)
- Filipino citizen.
- Incoming or current first-year student.
- Grade 12 GWA of at least 93%.
- Income of ₱500,000 or less.
- Not a TES, TDP or other national government grantee. One-time grants are exempt, and the label now says so.

## Added rules
- **Has not finished an undergraduate degree:** no second-course takers (Section 5.7).
- **Not a transferee or shiftee with credited units** (Section 5.5). This reuses the existing "college units in another program" question.
- **Graduated from a Philippine senior high school** (Section 4.2): a required *confirm*, because BAKR does not ask this.
- **The priority program is now required** (Section 5.4: non-priority programs cannot apply). It stays a *confirm*, because the priority list was not in the pages seen.

## Information updated
- **Benefits**, for state colleges (Section 13.1):
  - Free Higher Education TOSF;
  - Full: ₱40,000 per semester (₱35,000 stipend + ₱5,000 book/connectivity);
  - Half: ₱20,000 per semester (₱17,500 + ₱2,500).
- **Documents:** the Section 6 list, word for word.
- **Renewal requirements per term:** the Section 16.1.2 "succeeding payment" documents.
  - These feed the provider's Requirements page.
  - The seeder and the sync command write them only when config lists them, so provider-entered ones are kept.
- **How to apply:** Section 28.3 (CHEDRO evaluates and ranks, notifies by SMS or e-mail, issues the NOA).
- **Source note:** how to keep the scholarship (Section 17.3: GWA of 85% for full, 80% for half; regular load; finish on time).
- **Official link:** ched.gov.ph instead of the legacy page.

**Not modelled:** full vs half is decided by CHEDRO ranking (Section 28.3.3). The pages seen give no GWA split, so BAKR does not guess one.

## Database
Preview on the real `bakr` DB:
- 10 fields differ;
- rules `no_degree`, `no_credited_units` and `ph_shs` added;
- 4 forms to re-check.

**Not applied yet.**

## Tests
126/126 pass. New: CMSP excludes degree holders, students with credited units, and a GWA below 93%.

# CHED TES updated from the Revised TES Guidelines of 2026 (+ unifast.gov.ph/tes.html)

## The key fix
- **Before:** BAKR made "Listahanan or 4Ps" a *required* rule, so every student outside those groups was shown "not eligible".
- **What Section 3 says:** they are the **first priority**. "All other enrolled students" follow: first dependents of solo parents, then Indigenous Peoples, ranked by household per capita income and subject to funds.
- **Now:** the priority rule is **not required**. It covers Listahanan, 4Ps, solo-parent dependents and Indigenous Peoples. Students in a priority group pass it and rank higher; others show "Nearly eligible" instead of being excluded.

## Other changes
- **New required rules:**
  - enrolled at DNSC (non-students are disqualified);
  - not already a TDP, CHED scholarship or other national aid beneficiary (one-time grants such as AICS and SMART do not count).
- **Benefits for state colleges:**
  - Listahanan/4Ps: ₱10,000 per semester (₱20,000 a year);
  - solo-parent dependents and Indigenous Peoples: ₱5,000 per semester;
  - PWD (TES-3A): +₱5,000 per semester;
  - TES-3B licensure: up to ₱8,000 one-time;
  - SARDO: ₱10,000 one-time;
  - all for the standard program length + 1 year.
- **Documents:** COR/COE; a school or government ID with specimen signature (when submitting directly to CHEDRO); PWD ID if applicable.
- **Renewal requirements:** COR/COE each semester; approved LOA if returning after one.
- **How to apply:** DNSC submits enrolled applicants to the UniFAST Portal; CHEDRO and the RCs validate and issue the Master List. Students may submit directly to CHEDRO XI.

## Tests and database
- **Tests:** 126/126 pass.
  - `test_tes_needs_listahanan_or_4ps` was replaced by a test that priority groups rank higher without excluding others.
  - The requirements test now expects TES to have renewal requirements.
- **Database:** the TES preview on the real DB was run. **Not applied yet.**

## Source PDFs
The new `docs/sources/README.md` indexes every official document used. The CHED PDFs go in `docs/sources/ched/`.

## Rules

- Filipino citizen;
- incoming first-year;
- Top 5 of the class **or** a GWA of at least 95%;
- no college units;
- income of ₱2,000,000 or less.

**Labels sharpened:**
- "foreign or dual citizens cannot apply" (Section 3.1);

- **The priority program is now required** (Section 5.1), and stays a *confirm*.

**Added, optional:**
- **Special equity group** (Section 8.7c and footnote 5): Indigenous Peoples, PWD, solo parent or dependent, living in poverty (Listahanan/4Ps), or first-generation student.

  - ₱45,000 per academic year stipend (₱22,500 per semester);

- **Documents:** Sections 4 and 6.
- **Renewal requirements:** Section 10.2.2, succeeding payments.
- **Application route:** Section 8 (portal, CHEDRO, ranking, NOA within 15 days, choose one if qualified for two national programs).
- **Source note:** retention and return service (Sections 18.1.2 and 18.5).
- **Replaces:** the old "from the BAKR scholarships page" note.
