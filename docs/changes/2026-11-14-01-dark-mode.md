# 2026-11-14 — Light / dark mode with a theme switch

## What it does
- A **switch** (sun · moon track with a gold knob, `role="switch"` labelled "Dark mode") sits in:
  - the public navbar (with the words "Dark mode" in the phone menu);
  - the student and provider portal headers (next to the round icons);
  - every login page (next to "Back to home").
- **The choice is remembered** per browser (`localStorage` `bakr.theme`). Until someone chooses, BAKR follows the device's light or dark setting, live.
- **No white flash:** `public/js/bakr-theme.js` loads in `<head>` (not deferred) and sets `html[data-theme]` before the first paint.
- **Smooth switching:** the page cross-fades (View Transitions API) unless reduced motion is on.
- **Scope:** public pages, the Finder, the login pages, the student portal (including the match dialogs) and the provider portal. The static staff prototypes (admin/officer/scholar) stay light.

## How (light mode untouched)
- **`public/css/bakr-dark.css`:** a new file loaded **last** on every page shell. Every dark rule is under `:root[data-theme="dark"]`.
  - The dark palette is deep green-black surfaces, warm off-white text and the same gold accents.
  - Brand logos keep their white discs.
- **Role colours.** `--green-900`, `--green-700` and `--vanilla` were each used both as text and as fills, so their text and page-background uses were renamed (147 places):
  - `--text-strong` (headings)
  - `--text-accent`
  - `--page-bg` / `--page-bg-deep`
  - `--dash-text-strong` (dashboards)

  In light mode these are defined as exactly the old values (`var(--green-900)` and so on), so light mode renders identically. Checked by screenshot.
- **Solid gold elements keep fixed dark-green text** in both themes (26 rules restored to `--green-900`). Examples:
  - gold buttons;
  - the active nav link;
  - "Find a Scholarship";
  - sidebar badges.
- **Hard-coded whites** (cards, tables, inputs, dialogs, pills, the dropdown, section bands) get dark versions. They were found by listing every white or near-white background in the 11 stylesheets.
- **Mistake fixed during the check:** overriding the Scholarships page's `--sd-green-900`, `--sd-vanilla` and `--sd-surface` flipped the "Apply now" button and the sector bar, because those variables are also used as text colours. They are now left alone, and the cards are styled directly.
