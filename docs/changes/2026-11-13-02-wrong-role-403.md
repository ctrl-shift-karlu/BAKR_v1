# 2026-11-13 — Explain 403s when signed in with the wrong account

**Problem:** opening `/student/application` showed a bare "403 Forbidden". The browser was still signed in as the CHED **provider** account. Student pages require `role:student`, so the refusal itself was correct, but nothing said why. The public navbar also still showed "Sign in as" and "Find a Scholarship", which hid the fact that a provider was signed in.

## Changed
- `resources/views/partials/navbar.blade.php`: a signed-in provider sees a **Provider account** menu (Provider dashboard, Sign out), and the "Find a Scholarship" button is hidden for them.

## Added
- `resources/views/errors/403.blade.php`: still a 403 (access rules unchanged). It names the kind of account that is signed in, explains that student pages need a student account (or provider pages a provider account), and offers "Go to my dashboard" and **Sign out**.
- `tests/Feature/WrongRolePageTest.php` (2 tests).
